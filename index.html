WAP Browser

The mobile web of the early 2000s, on Android

WAP Browser opens WAP sites: pages written in WML, with WBMP images and WMLScript. It reads WML 1.3 and binary WBXML, runs WMLScript, and connects over HTTP, HTTPS or a WAP gateway (WSP).

Try it. Open this address in WAP Browser:
jabezwinston.github.io/wap/wap/index.wml
