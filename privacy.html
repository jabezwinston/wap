Privacy Policy

WAP Browser for Android · Effective 4 November 2026

WAP Browser (package io.github.jabezwinston.wapbrowser) is developed by Jabez Winston. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the developer collects nothing.

Information the developer collects

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. The developer runs no server that the app talks to and never receives your data.

Information stored on your device

To work as a browser, the app keeps the following on your device only:

  • Your open tabs, browsing history and Home shortcuts
  • Cookies set by the sites you visit
  • Your settings, such as theme, zoom and WAP gateway address

You can clear this from within the app (for example, Clear Cookies), or remove all of it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Android may include this data in your device's own backup if you have backup turned on; that backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by the developer.

Sites you visit

When you open a page, the app connects directly to that site, or to the WAP gateway you have configured, and sends what a browser normally sends: the address requested, your IP address, the app's user-agent, cookies for that site and anything you enter into a form on it. Those sites and gateways are run by third parties and their own privacy policies apply. Pages using plain http:// or a WAP gateway are not encrypted.

Files

The app only reads a file when you open it with WAP Browser, and only saves a file (such as a page or image) to a location you choose in Android's file picker.

Permissions

  • Internet, to load the pages you ask for.
  • Network state, which the built-in web view uses to know whether the device is online.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
